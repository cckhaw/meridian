# Document management

One vault for **identity documents (KYC)** and **signed agreements**, an editor for the **agreement templates** with legal versions, and the **signature stamping** that turns a template plus an order into a sealed PDF. Code: `packages/core/src/{documents,doc-store,doc-templates,doc-html,doc-pdf,agreements}.ts`, tables in `packages/db/migrations/0054_documents.sql`.

> **How this maps to the stack Meridian actually runs.** The brief named NestJS, Prisma, BullMQ/Temporal, Kafka and PDFKit/Puppeteer. Meridian is Next.js route handlers over `@meridian/core` (raw SQL on PostgreSQL), so: the services are plain modules in `@meridian/core`; the Prisma models are SQL tables (`documents`, `document_templates`, `template_versions`, `document_signatures`, `document_audit_log`; identity documents are rows of `documents` with `kind = 'KYC'`); PDFs are made with `pdf-lib` and the Inter typeface already used for invoices (no headless browser, so it runs on Vercel); the queue is the existing outbox (`lifecycle_outbox`) and the `CONTRACT_GENERATED` event goes through the integration hub like every other event (webhooks, TM Forum 688 listeners, topics) rather than directly to Kafka.

## Storage and security
- **Where bytes live.** In the database by default; in any S3-compatible bucket (AWS S3, MinIO, GCS interoperability, R2) when `MERIDIAN_DOCS_S3_ENDPOINT`, `_BUCKET`, `_ACCESS_KEY`, `_SECRET_KEY` (and optionally `_REGION`, `_PATH_STYLE`) are set. Requests are signed with AWS Signature V4 in `doc-store.ts` (tested against AWS's published example). Older documents stay readable wherever they were written.
- **Identity documents are encrypted before storage** with AES-256-GCM (key derived from `MERIDIAN_DATA_KEY`, as for all identity data). The recorded SHA-256 is of the plain bytes and is checked every time a file is opened; a file that no longer matches never opens.
- **Links.** Nobody gets a public URL. A person gets a signed link that works for **15 minutes** (a bucket link for unencrypted contracts, otherwise `/api/documents/file/<token>`); issuing and using one is logged.
- **Access log.** `document_audit_log` is append-only (the database refuses to change or delete a line). It records every upload, view, download, link, decision, template edit/publish, signature, delivery, purge and integrity failure with who, from which IP and browser, and when.
- **Permissions.** `document:read` (signed agreements), `document:kyc` (identity documents and signature images: view, upload, verify, reject), `document:manage` (purge, retention, sweep), `document:template` (write and publish templates). Compliance holds all four; customer-service roles hold `document:read`; the administrator holds `document:template`.

## Identity vault
Categories: `NATIONAL_ID_FRONT`, `NATIONAL_ID_BACK`, `PASSPORT`, `UTILITY_BILL`, `CORPORATE_REGISTRATION_SSM`, `PROOF_OF_ADDRESS` (the store-front also adds `SELFIE` and `CUSTOMER_SIGNATURE`). Lifecycle: `PENDING_VERIFICATION` → `VERIFIED_AUTO` / `VERIFIED_MANUAL` → `REJECTED` → `EXPIRED` → `PURGED_RETENTION`. Evidence collected by the store-front's KYC flow is in the vault too (its bytes stay in `kyc_documents`; the vault line follows the case decision, `VERIFIED_AUTO` when the check was automatic). Staff upload and verify documents in **Documents**.

**Retention** (Settings → Documents; the daily sweep `POST /api/documents/sweep`, also run by `POST /api/jobs/sweep-orders`): an upload that never reached a customer is removed after 48 hours (configurable); a verified document past its own expiry becomes `EXPIRED`; when a customer's last service has been terminated, their identity documents are kept N years (default 7) and agreements N years, then purged (bytes removed, the line and its history stay). Nothing is purged while the customer has a live service.

## Agreement templates
Written in **Settings → Document templates** (`/settings/document-templates`): a rich-text editor over a safe subset of HTML (headings, paragraphs, emphasis, lists, simple tables; everything else is stripped on save), a token picker, a live preview with sample data, and version history with a line-by-line comparison.
- **Tokens:** `{{customer.fullName|nationalId|address|email|phone}}`, `{{order.orderNumber|createdDate|planName|monthlyFee|upfrontAmount|contractMonths|items}}`, `{{service.msisdn|iccid|imei}}`, `{{change.fromPlan|toPlan|effectiveDate}}`, `{{signature.customerImage|timestamp|agentName}}`, `{{company.name}}`. An unknown token is refused at save time. `nationalId` is the masked number; a value not known yet at signing (a number assigned on activation) prints as such.
- **Versions:** one working draft, then **published** versions `v1.0`, `v1.1` (minor: wording) or `v2.0` (major: something new to agree to). Publishing retires the previous one. Only published versions are ever signed, and a signed document keeps the id of the exact version (`documents.template_version_id`), so later edits never change what a customer agreed to.
- **Where each applies:** a template lists the contexts it is signed in (`ORDER`, `PLAN_CHANGE`) and the offer types it applies to (none = every offer).

## Signing at an order and at a plan change
- **Which agreements apply.** Every active template whose contexts include the situation (`ORDER` or `PLAN_CHANGE`) and whose offer types match the basket (or all, if none are listed). *Settings → Documents* has two switches, **require signature at order** and **at plan change** (off by default: with them off an order or change with no signature simply has no agreement). Quick sales at a counter and any order sent without a signature pad are not asked.
- **New order (CRM).** At the Review step the screen fetches the agreements (`POST /api/orders/agreements`, same channel permission as the quote), shows the text with the customer's own details, and takes the signature on a drawing pad (finger, stylus or mouse) after "I have read". The order (`POST /api/orders`, field `agreement`) then makes one sealed PDF per agreement, stored and bound to the order with the exact version signed, the signer, the device address (IP), the browser, the location if the person allowed it, and the staff member who took it. If a new version was published while the text was on screen, the sale is refused with "read the new text and sign again".
- **New order (store-front).** The shopper reads and signs on the Review step; the signature is kept encrypted on the application (`applications.agreement`) until checkout makes the order, so the IP and browser stored are the shopper's. Identity documents they upload are in the vault too and follow the check (`VERIFIED_AUTO` when the automatic check approved them).
- **Plan change.** In the customer's service panel, **Change plan**: choose the new plan and when it takes effect, read and sign the plan change agreement; `POST /api/services/:id/change-plan` stores the signed PDF with the service before anything moves. Partners can do the same through the northbound API (`change-plan` takes `agreement`; `orders` takes `agreement`); a partner that does not send one is refused when the switch is on.
- **After signing.** `CONTRACT_GENERATED` is raised (webhooks, TM Forum 688 listeners, topics) with the document id, the order or service, the template and version. The customer's copy is queued in the notification outbox as `CONTRACT_SIGNED` (once the order is paid, or at once for a plan change) with the document ids; the sender asks `GET /api/v1/customers/{id}/documents` (scope `document:read`) for fresh 15-minute links when it sends. A cancelled or failed order marks its agreements `SUPERSEDED`.
- **The customer's own copy.** `GET /api/me/documents` (self-care portal) and `GET /api/v1/customers/{customerId}/documents` list a customer's signed agreements with a 15-minute link each; identity documents are never listed there.

## Screens
- **Documents** (`/documents`, permission `document:read` and/or `document:kyc`): search by customer name, order or customer id; filter by kind and status; counts of what is waiting. A document opens with its details (customer, status and who decided, expiry, retention, where it is stored, and for a contract the signer, device address, location and seal), the actions the person may take (view, download through a 15-minute link, check a contract is untouched, verify or reject an identity document with a reason, remove under retention), and **who has been here**: the access log for that document. Viewing shows the file from its own address under a watermark naming the viewer and the time. Identity documents can be added for a customer here. The customer's page shows their documents too.
- **Document templates** (`/settings/document-templates`, permission `document:template`): the list of templates with the live version and any unpublished draft; a rich-text editor (headings, emphasis, lists, quotes, rules, alignment; pasting brings plain text only) with a token picker; **Preview** with sample details; **Versions** with who published what and when, how many signed documents each has, and a line-by-line comparison of any two; **Where it applies** (contexts, offer types, in use or not). Saving keeps a draft; **Publish** asks minor or major and shows the number it will become.
- **Settings → Documents**: signature required at order and at plan change, send signed copies, and the retention periods.
- The PDF viewer inside the page needs the browser's own PDF support; **Download** always opens the file in its own tab. Two addresses (`/api/documents/:id/file` and the link form) are the only ones the CRM allows itself to frame.

## Identity photographs

Identity photographs (ID front/back, selfie, company certificate) are vault documents of kind `KYC`, linked to their case by `kyc_case_id`, encrypted, status *pending verification* until the case is decided (then verified or rejected). See [KYC.md](KYC.md).
